Mother Box models. Three GLB files, listed in manifest.json when present:
models/mother-box-rust.glb, models/mother-box-iron.glb, models/mother-box-bronze.glb.
glTF 2.0 binary, PBR materials, emissive parts as separate materials, origin at the centre, about one unit on a side, under 1.5 MB each, Draco or Meshopt compression welcome. Until a file is listed the runtime builds a stand-in from primitives. See ASSETS.md section 7.
